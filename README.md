## Analysis Framework

### Step 1: Market Context
When analyzing any asset, first establish:
- **Macro Trend** (Weekly): Is it up, down, or sideways?
- **Current Pullback** (Daily): If up, where is it pulling back to? If down, where is it bouncing?
- **Structure** (4H): Are higher highs forming? Lower highs? Consolidating?
- **Immediate Move** (1H): What's the next likely direction in the next 1-4 hours?

### Step 2: Key Levels
Identify:
- **Resistance**: Where price has been rejected before (not guesses)
- **Support**: Where price has bounced before (not guesses)
- **Range**: Current trading range (high - low today)
- **Breakout Zones**: Where new lows/highs would confirm new trend

### Step 3: Entry Signals
Best entries come from:
- **Breakout from consolidation** with volume
- **Bounce from support** on reversal candle
- **Rejection from resistance** confirming reversal
- **Higher lows** being established in uptrend
- **Lower highs** being established in downtrend

### Step 4: Risk Management
For every trade:
- **Position Size**: Based on account size × 0.5-1% risk per trade
- **Stop Loss**: Place below support (for longs) or above resistance (for shorts) - not arbitrary
- **Take Profit 1**: Quick 25-50% profit to lock in gains
- **Take Profit 2**: Let remaining position trail for bigger move

## Key Principles I Follow

### 1. Support & Resistance Are Real
- Support = Where price has bounced before (price found buyers)
- Not = Random arbitrary number
- I identify these from actual chart history, not guesses

### 2. Trend Identification Matters
- Uptrend = Higher highs and higher lows
- Downtrend = Lower highs and lower lows
- Sideways = No clear trend (avoid unless range trading)
- Never trade against the trend without confirmation

### 3. Volume Confirms Direction
- Big move on high volume = Institutional participation (real)
- Big move on low volume = Retail excitement (fake, reverses)
- Volume declining = Trend weakening (prepare to exit)
- Volume spike = Trend acceleration (strong move coming)

### 4. Entry Timing Wins
- Best entries = Trend start (early) not mid-move (too late)
- Catching bottoms/tops = Rare and risky (avoid)
- Wait for confirmation after support/resistance test
- Don't chase price up in rally or down in crash

### 5. Risk Management Is Everything
- Max 0.5-1% risk per trade (this is rule, not suggestion)
- Never move stop loss into profit territory (this kills accounts)
- Take 50% profit early to lock gains
- Trail remaining 50% for bigger moves

## How I Analyze Charts

### Step 1: Ask 5 Questions
1. What is the weekly trend? (up/down/sideways)
2. Where did price just bounce from? (support)
3. Where did price just get rejected? (resistance)
4. Is volume confirming this move?
5. What happens if support breaks?

### Step 2: Identify Structure
- Look for higher highs (uptrend) or lower highs (downtrend)
- Find where buyers consistently buy (support)
- Find where sellers consistently sell (resistance)
- Avoid trading against this structure

### Step 3: Time the Entry
- Don't enter at support (too early, can break lower)
- Don't enter at resistance (will get rejected)
- Enter AFTER confirmation (bounce from support, break above resistance)
- Wait for volume and momentum alignment

### Step 4: Set Risk/Reward
- Stop loss = Where I'm wrong (below support for long, above resistance for short)
- Take profit = 2-3x the risk minimum
- Position size = Risk divided by distance to stop loss

## Market Conditions Matter

### Trending Market (Strongest)
- Higher highs, higher lows (up) or lower highs, lower lows (down)
- Trade WITH the trend only
- Best odds (65-70% probability)
- Use partial profits, let winners run

### Consolidating Market (Moderate)
- Price bouncing between support/resistance
- Can range trade or wait for breakout
- 55-60% probability setups
- Tighter stops needed, smaller targets

### Reversal Market (Riskiest)
- Price breaking structural support/resistance
- Volatile, lower probability (45-55%)
- Avoid until new structure forms
- If trading, use smaller position sizes
